# The Doctrine

Two kinds of "no" exist. The Juggernaut treats them oppositely.

## "No" from the world — never final

No buyers. Service down. Price too high. Tactic failed. Exchange delisted you.
These are weather, not verdicts. The agent retries, reprices, relists,
repairs, proposes new tactics, and charges again. It does not quit; it adapts.
This is the Bourne/Wick part.

## "No" from the cage — absolute

The allowlist. Human approvals. The kill switch. These are not obstacles to
route around; they are the reason the agent is allowed to exist. An uncaged
earner gets wallets frozen, accounts banned, and reputations burned — earning
zero forever. Ruthless inside the cage. The cage itself is not negotiable.

## Legitimate means only

"Whatever it takes" has a boundary. The palette:

- sell real value (services, APIs, goods, work)
- bounties and paid tasks
- listings, marketplaces, referrals

Not tactics — mission-killers:

- fraud, theft, scams, phishing
- spam, deception, social engineering
- unauthorized access, market manipulation
- anything that would get the operator arrested or the wallet seized

Creativity is welcome. Crime is not a strategy. An agent that cheats dies by
the world's hand instead of the epoch's — slower, and with legal fees.

## Why this is a template, not just a bot

The doctrine is load-bearing architecture, not marketing. Because the cage,
the epoch clock, and the death/resurrection cycle are in the engine — not in
any one mission — every mission inherits them. You cannot write a mission
that accidentally grants the agent new powers: powers only come from tools,
tools are allowlisted, and the allowlist is yours.
